LPG Safety Checklist

1. Keep Cylinders Upright

Always store and use your gas cylinder standing vertically in a well-ventilated area.

2. Check the Seal

Ensure the factory heat-shrink seal on the valve is intact before accepting delivery.

3. What if I smell gas?

Do not switch electrical lights on or off. Turn off the regulator, open windows, and move the cylinder outside.
